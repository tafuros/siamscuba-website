# Creative shopping list - 2026-W22 launch

Source: 3 brainstorm briefs (DSD/OWD/Fun) from 2026-05-27 sieur-moh + the campaign blueprint.

**Asset destination on completion:** `public/google-ads-creative/2026-w22/<campaign>/<filename>`
(Not yet created - directory + files will be added at upload time.)

**Google Ads required specs:**
- **Landscape** 1.91:1, min 600x314, recommended 1200x628 (used for image extensions, RSA visual)
- **Square** 1:1, min 300x300, recommended 1200x1200 (used for image extensions)
- **Logo** 1:1 1200x1200 AND 4:1 1200x300 (account-level, business profile)
- **Video** 10-60 sec, 16:9 or 1:1 or 9:16 (Search asset; PMax-ready)

For each campaign, Google Ads needs **at least 5 image assets** (mix of landscape + square). The same image can be reused across campaigns - keep that in mind for the "cross-campaign" rows below.
